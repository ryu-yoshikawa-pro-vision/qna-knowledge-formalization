Design the login screen for an internal organizational knowledge formalization platform.

Goal:
Create a simple, trustworthy entry point for users signing in with Google.

Requirements:
- Centered login layout
- Product name
- Short value proposition explaining that the platform turns internal questions, answers, and direct posts into reusable formal knowledge
- Primary “Continue with Google” button
- Minimal supporting text about workspace-based access
- Clean and restrained visual tone
- No signed-in navigation yet
- No distracting illustrations or marketing-heavy visuals

Design intent:
This should feel operational, credible, and calm.
It should feel like access to an internal enterprise system, not a public consumer app.
